## English

**Licence.** CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (SPDX `CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0`). Full legal text: [LICENSE](LICENSE).

### Allowed without asking

1. **Refer to the project.** You may use the unmodified marks and wordmark to link to, review,
   report on, or discuss DSH Link — for example in an article, a video, a package listing, or a
   downstream project's "works with DSH Link" statement — provided you do not imply endorsement.
2. **Personal / non-commercial use and modification** under the same licence, with attribution
   and a clear note that you changed the artwork.
3. **Redistribution together with DSH Link** (including in the source repository and the installers),
   unchanged, under this same licence.

### Not allowed without permission

1. **Commercial use** of the artwork (selling it, using it as a product or service identity, or in
   paid advertising) — the NC term.
2. **Trademark use**: registering the DSH Link name or a confusingly similar mark, or using the
   artwork as the identity of your own product or organisation.
3. **Implying endorsement or affiliation** with DSH Link or its contributors, or with DeepSeek /
   DeepSeek Harness (DSH Link is a third-party desktop edition and has no official affiliation).
4. **Derivative branding** that keeps the artwork recognisable while presenting a different name or
   product (a recolour or a small edit on top of the mark is still derivative branding).
5. **AI training of a mark-generation model** on this artwork for commercial output.

Commercial licensing, permission for an adapted mark, and press questions: open an issue in this
repository.

### Attribution format

> "DSH Link brand artwork" by DSH Link contributors, licensed under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.
> Source: https://github.com/DreamRealm-Dev/dsh-link-brand. Modified versions must be marked as modified.

In non-markdown contexts, keep the title, the author, the licence name and the source URL.

### Where it applies in the main repository

The DSH Link source repository is MIT for code, but the copies of this artwork under
`plugins/dsh-link-brand/assets/`, `src-tauri/icons/` (renders of the mark) and the boot-page mark are
covered by CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. See the main repository's `BRAND-ASSETS.md`.

## 简体中文

**许可。** CC BY-NC-SA 4.0（SPDX `CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0`）。完整法律文本见 [LICENSE](LICENSE)。

### 无需询问即可

1. **指向本项目**：可原样使用标志与字标来链接、评测、报道或讨论 DSH Link（文章、视频、包列表、
   下游项目的“可与 DSH Link 配合使用”说明等），但不得暗示获得背书。
2. **个人 / 非商业使用与改编**：需署名、以同一许可发布，并清楚标注你修改过素材。
3. **随 DSH Link 一起分发**（源码仓库与安装包内），原样、以同一许可。

### 未经许可不得

1. **商业使用**素材（出售、作为产品或服务标识、用于付费广告）——NC 条款。
2. **商标性使用**：把 DSH Link 名称或易混淆的近似标志注册为商标，或把素材当作你自己产品或组织的标识。
3. **暗示与 DSH Link 及其贡献者、或与 DeepSeek / DeepSeek Harness 存在背书或关联**
   （DSH Link 是第三方桌面版，与官方无隶属关系）。
4. **衍生品牌**：在保留可识别性的前提下换成别的名称或产品（在标志上改色、小改仍然算衍生品牌）。
5. 用本素材**训练商业用途的标志生成模型**。

商业授权、改编授权与媒体询问：请在本仓库开 issue。
